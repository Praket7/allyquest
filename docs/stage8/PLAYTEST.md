# First independent-child playtest protocol — not yet conducted

Target: 12–20 voluntary participants ages 7–12, with guardian consent, including children with and without allergies, to avoid single-population bias. Do not collect allergy diagnoses unnecessarily.

At least two children should independently play Stage 7 and two Stage 8 before any coaching; counterbalance order for the remaining participants. Give only "Please play this game" and do not explain mechanics that the interface should teach.

Log (on a paper or de-identified local sheet): 1) whether the child notices Maya's speech bubble; 2) first attempt to talk; 3) whether they can advance the bubble on their device; 4) whether they notice the optional choice; 5) mistakes and frustrations; 6) whether they understand the banner objective; 7) number of real trampoline bounces and respawns; 8) whether they independently choose another attempt; 9) what they believe friends should do about shared food safety.

Counterfactual test: ask a simple non-game scenario question about including a friend at a school event before and after play. No clinical claims or personalized advice are made.

Gate recommendations, not measured data: >85% discover Maya's bubble; >85% understand how to continue dialogue; >75% find the banner without instructor intervention; no recurring unreadable mobile bubbles; no critical safety misconception. Revise based on observations, not an arbitrary score alone.
